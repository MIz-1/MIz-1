## What I Build

---

## Projects

### Phase 1 — Aerodynamic Drag Simulator
Simulates how air resistance affects vehicle speed and acceleration.
Physics: F_drag = 0.5 x rho x Cd x A x v squared
[View Project](https://github.com/MIz-1/drag-simulator)

### Phase 2 — AI Smart Suspension System
Quarter-car model with AI PD controller vs passive suspension.
Physics: Newton second law + PD control theory
[View Project](https://github.com/MIz-1/ai-suspension-system)

### Phase 3 — KERS Energy Recovery System
Kinetic energy capture during braking, boost during acceleration.
Physics: KE = 0.5 x m x v squared, P = F x v
[View Project](https://github.com/MIz-1/kers-simulation)

### Phase 4 — Combined Simulation
All three systems working together in one unified model.
[View Project](https://github.com/MIz-1/combined-simulation)
